Methodology & limitations

What this prototype does—and what it does not claim.

Feature engineering

1, 7, 14 and 28-day lag values, rolling averages, calendar fields, price and promotion flags.

Validation

Chronological 28-day holdout. Reported metrics use a rolling one-step backtest with observed previous lags.

Recursive outlook

Future days use earlier predictions as lag inputs. Multi-day forecast error may therefore be higher.

Inventory assumptions

On-hand stock, lead time and safety stock are editable inputs. The bundled sample inventory is synthetic.
